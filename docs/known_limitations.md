## Analytical Limitations
*   **Speaking Time vs. Influence**: The application measures speaking time and turn frequency. These are quantitative metrics of participation. The system *does not* equate speaking time with influence, competence, leadership, or negotiation success.
*   **Behavior vs. Intent**: The LLM classifies observed communication behavior (e.g., "Negotiation", "Agreement"). It does not claim to know a participant's private motives, psychological state, or deceptive intent. 
*   **Blue Index**: The "Blue Index" remains a proposed concept and is currently excluded from the core analytics dashboard until a statistically validated formula is approved.
